# Telemetry

Logs from human playtests, read by the `designer` to revise rooms. Suggested
format, one file per session: `telemetry/YYYY-MM-DD_<name>.md` or `.csv` with
room id, deaths, seconds, and free-text notes. Do not put ROM data, savestates
or screenshots with ROM-derived content here unless they are small and you are
comfortable committing them (screenshots of a ROM hack are fine to keep local;
the default is to keep them out of git).
